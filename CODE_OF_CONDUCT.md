# Code of Conduct

Syncmoney welcomes technical discussion, bug reports, documentation work, testing, and code contributions from people with different levels of experience and backgrounds. Participation should remain respectful, useful, and safe for the people involved.

Traditional Chinese: [`docs/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh_tw.md`](docs/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh_tw.md)

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and code on their technical merits;
- assume good faith while still asking for evidence when claims affect correctness or security;
- give actionable review feedback and accept corrections without personal attacks;
- respect different experience levels, languages, time zones, accessibility needs, and communication styles;
- keep private reports, credentials, player data, and other confidential material private;
- stop behavior that a maintainer reasonably identifies as disruptive or unsafe;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when they materially affect a technical or moderation decision.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or sustained personal attacks;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments based on personal characteristics;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention;
- deliberate outing, doxxing, or publication of private information;
- knowingly publishing credentials, private vulnerability details, or sensitive player/server data without authorization;
- spam, repeated off-topic disruption, impersonation, or deliberate sabotage of collaboration;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith conduct or security concern.

Technical disagreement, firm code review, closing an unsupported request, or asking for reproducible evidence is not by itself misconduct when it remains focused on the work and is communicated professionally.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-controlled spaces, including repository issues and pull requests, review discussions, project community channels, and project events. It also applies when someone is clearly representing Syncmoney in another space.

## Reporting conduct concerns

When possible, report a conduct concern privately to the project maintainers through the official project community at <https://official.noie.fun>. Do not publish private personal information in a public issue in order to make a report.

For a security vulnerability, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead of posting exploit details publicly.

Provide the relevant links/messages, dates, participants, and a concise description of what happened. Only include private information that is necessary for the maintainers to understand the report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take proportionate action based on severity, context, prior behavior, and impact. Actions can include a private warning, removing or editing abusive content, limiting participation, closing discussions, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from project-controlled spaces.

Maintainers should avoid conflicts of interest when handling reports, share details only with people who need them to resolve the matter, and document serious enforcement decisions internally where practical. Reports made in bad faith may themselves be treated as a conduct issue.
